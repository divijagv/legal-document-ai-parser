UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
WESTERN DISTRICT OF WASHINGTON AT SEATTLE

Case No. 3:26-cv-01922
Harborline Logistics LLC v. Pacific Northwest Data Corp.

ORDER COMPELLING PRODUCTION OF RECORDS

THIS MATTER comes before the Court on Plaintiff's motion to compel. Having considered the motion and the record, the Court ORDERS as follows:

1. Defendant Pacific Northwest Data Corp. shall produce to Plaintiff's counsel all server access logs, account configuration records, and billing records associated with the customer account "harborline_llc" (Account ID: PND-2231-8874).

2. The production shall cover the period from February 1, 2025 through May 31, 2026.

3. Production shall be completed no later than August 25, 2026.

4. Sensitive credentials and unrelated third-party customer data shall be redacted prior to production.

IT IS SO ORDERED.

Dated this 9th day of July, 2026.
